## CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR - REMOVAL AND INSTALLATION

The camshaft position sensor is mounted to the rear of the cylinder head on bank 1.

1. Disconnect the negative battery cable.
2. Disconnect the camshaft position sensor harness connector.
3. Remove the sensor retaining bolt.
4. Remove the sensor from the cylinder head.

Installation is the reverse of removal. Tighten the camshaft position sensor retaining
bolt to 9 Nm using bolt size M6 x 1.0. Do not reuse the O-ring.

## DTC P0340 CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT

DTC P0340 is stored when the ECM detects no camshaft position signal during cranking.
Possible causes include an open or shorted signal circuit, a failed sensor, damage to
the reluctor ring, or timing chain stretch.

Diagnostic procedure: with the ignition on and the engine off, check for 5 volts
reference at the sensor connector. If reference voltage is absent, test the harness
between the sensor and the ECM for continuity.

## CYLINDER HEAD - TIGHTENING SEQUENCE

Tighten the cylinder head bolts in sequence shown in the illustration. Stage 1: torque
all bolts to 40 Nm. Stage 2: tighten an additional 90 degrees. Cylinder head bolts are
torque-to-yield and must be replaced. Bolt size M11 x 1.5.

## LABOR TIME - CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR REPLACEMENT

Labor time for camshaft position sensor replacement, bank 1: 0.7 hrs. Warranty time
for the same operation is 0.5 hrs.

## WIRING - CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR CIRCUIT

The camshaft position sensor connector is a three pin connector. Pin 1 is the 5 volt
reference, wire colour white. Pin 2 is the signal circuit, wire colour red. Pin 3 is
the sensor ground, wire colour black. The signal circuit runs to ECM connector E12
pin 44.
